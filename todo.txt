R. Precision of the numbers in the files
----------------------------------------
Checked on the 7 Gaussian files and the 4 CFOUR folders. The check scripts were one-off; the numbers
are here. How many digits each value the readers use has in the file:
    Gaussian:
        harmonic and anharmonic energies: 3 decimals (cm-1). B: 7 decimals. Fine.
        dipgrad, diphess, polgrad, polhess: 6 significant digits (D format). Fine.
        cff_rc, qff_rc: column FI, 5 decimals, on values at least 300x larger than in column K. Fine.
        cff, qff (au): converted from FI since R1. Column K (5 decimals) was not fine for small terms.
        coriolis: the short list, 5 decimals (R3, dropped: negligible for wilson).
        Small force constants are not in the file at all (R2).
    CFOUR: force constants and dipole derivatives with 10 decimals, harmonic frequencies with 6. Fine.
[ ] R2  [decide] Gaussian leaves out every force constant with |K| < 3.71e-5 (Hartree amu^(-n/2) Bohr^(-n)). The
        cut-off is Gaussian's default, listed in each .out file under "Cut-offs for the present calculation",
        "Tolerances on Data", 3rd and 4th derivs. The smallest printed |K| is 4.0e-5 in every file, so the cut is on
        K. The reader gets 0 for a missing term, in cff and cff_rc alike. Gaussian's own VPT2 probably leaves the
        terms out too (to check).
        Quartic terms with 4 different indices are never computed, by Gaussian or CFOUR; VPT2 does not need them.
        The counts below leave them out.
            - formaldehyde: 22 of 56 cubic and 45 of 111 quartic terms printed. These are exactly the terms that
              C2v symmetry allows, so nothing nonzero is missing.
            - ACAC (lowest mode 75 cm-1): 540 of 1140 cubic and 1346 of 2925 quartic terms not printed.
              ACDM (lowest mode 46 cm-1): 9633 of 13244 cubic and 27165 of 37065 quartic terms not printed.
              Some are zero by symmetry; how many is not known.
        Why it matters: the cut is on K, but FI = K * (unit factor) / sqrt(w_i w_j w_k) grows for low-frequency
        modes. A term just under the cut could be up to 16 cm-1 in FI (ACAC cubic), 9 (ACAC quartic), 34 (ACDM
        cubic), 52 (ACDM quartic). Those are upper bounds; the median bound is 0.1-0.4 cm-1.
        Not a parser bug: the numbers are not in the file. Ways: lower the cut-off in the Gaussian input (find the
        ReadAnharm option in the Gaussian manual) and rerun; or accept the cut, and say so next to the data (e.g.
        return the cut-off in the dict). CFOUR: not known whether it leaves out small terms; for formaldehyde it
        lists the same 22 cubic and 45 quartic index sets as Gaussian.
        For wilson: cff feeds the EVV terms, cff_rc and qff_rc the VPT2. No effect on wilson's test molecules
        (formaldehyde loses nothing); possibly the largest remaining error for large, floppy molecules.
        First step: rerun ACAC with a lower cut-off; compare cff, qff and the VPT2 energies (and wilson's results)
        with the present run. Only then choose a way.

X. Mixing origins (wilson S8)
-----------------------------
[ ] X1  [decide] Rules for data from two origins in one run: the mode order (reindexing), the sign of each mode,
        the axis orientation. CFOUR and Gaussian formaldehyde differ in mode order and in the sign of 2 modes (T4);
        for the axes the identity fits, but a mirror plane makes a flipped axis fit equally, so a fit alone does
        not fix the axes. Inside one origin none of this matters.
        Mode order: both readers give ascending harmonic frequency (N1). That is not enough on its own: two close
        modes can swap between methods or basis sets (e.g. formaldehyde B1 1203 and B2 1269 cm-1), so mode k of two
        outputs can be different vibrations, with no error. Mixing still needs a match by vibration (T4's
        matching). Gaussian's A order (the Mulliken numbering, reindex_modes=False) would not swap this way, but
        CFOUR would need its own numbering from symmetry labels, and B1/B2 depend on the axis choice.
        An old problem comes back with wilson S8: the overwrite in wilson (wilson_data_obtainer,
        dict.update: a second origin's keys replace the first's, e.g. cff_rc, reindex_modes) [wilson]. The other
        one, the crash of A2, is fixed.
